# UltraCRE — Direct Commercial Property Buyer

UltraCRE buys commercial real estate nationwide from a base in Edina, Minnesota. We review opportunities across the United States, with a focus on markets that have a population of 50,000 or more within a five-mile radius, and we consider other locations case by case.

Property types include industrial, retail, office, flex, mixed-use, and multifamily, as well as related assets such as warehouses, retail centers, apartment buildings, vacant commercial buildings, and commercial land. We can consider fully leased, partially occupied, or vacant properties.

Submissions are handled confidentially. Owners and brokers can submit details at [https://ultracre.com/submit/](https://ultracre.com/submit/) or by emailing capital@ultracre.com. Our team reviews each submission and, when it fits our criteria, works toward clear terms and a closing timeline that can be tailored to the transaction. For more information, visit [https://ultracre.com/](https://ultracre.com/) or call (612) 444-9999.
